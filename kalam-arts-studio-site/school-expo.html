Frequently asked

Good to know before you enquire.

The studio can discuss science and STEM models, architectural or scale models, cultural displays, exhibition craft and other visual presentation builds. The brief and project requirements determine what is feasible.

A respiratory-system model appears in the supplied studio portfolio. For a new project, send the topic, reference material, required labels, approximate size and any school instructions so the build can be scoped appropriately.

Share the assignment or exhibition brief, reference pictures, dimensions, required components, deadline and where the model will be presented. This helps identify the scope before a quotation.

The studio can discuss cultural displays, themed miniature scenes and event presentation craft. Include the event theme, venue dimensions, installation date and any organiser guidelines.

Enquire as soon as you have the brief and target date. Feasibility depends on complexity, material availability, quantity and the studio schedule, so no completion date is confirmed until the scope is reviewed.
