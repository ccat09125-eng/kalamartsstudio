What to prepare before commissioning a 3D model

A strong model brief combines purpose, scale, dimensions, reference material and presentation requirements. Here is what to prepare before you enquire.

Start with the purpose

Explain what the model needs to communicate. Is it a school or university presentation, an exhibition display, an architectural concept, a product visualisation or a personal project? Purpose influences the level of detail and the materials that make sense.

Collect clear references

Share sketches, photos, drawings, AutoCAD exports or inspiration images. Mark what must be accurate and what can be interpreted creatively. If you have a school brief or judging criteria, include it.

Confirm scale and dimensions

Provide the maximum overall size and, when relevant, a preferred scale. Mention where the finished model will be displayed, transported or stored.

Discuss material and finish

Foam, PVC, resin and other materials behave differently. The final choice depends on structure, appearance, durability, detail and budget. Ask about finish, colour and any lighting or moving parts.

Set a realistic timeline

Include the final submission or installation date, along with time needed for review and changes. A detailed project may need concept approval before fabrication begins.

A simple brief you can copy

Project purpose: what should the model explain or display?

Reference material: photographs, sketches, PDF plans or AutoCAD exports.

Dimensions: maximum width, height and depth, plus preferred scale if known.

Materials and finish: any required materials, colour references or surface details.

Display and handover: where it will be placed, how it will be moved and the target date.

What makes a brief easier to assess?

Separate essential details from optional decoration. Mark any measurements or features that must be accurate, and identify areas where the studio can suggest a practical solution. If the model is for a class or exhibition, include the official instructions and display restrictions.
