Where can I request a custom pencil portrait in Abu Dhabi?

KALAM ARTS STUDIO creates custom portrait drawings from supplied reference photographs. Portrait enquiries can include an individual or family composition, a preferred size and a personal occasion or gift brief.

Send the clearest original photo you have, the number of people and the date you need the portrait.
