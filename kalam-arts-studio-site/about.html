About the studio

An Abu Dhabi creative studio for art, craft & making

KALAM ARTS STUDIO is an Abu Dhabi-based creative studio for custom artwork, drawing, 3D craft and visual projects. The work ranges from hand-painted canvas and portrait commissions to miniature models, school displays, murals and event visuals.

Talk to the studio ↗
Our approach

Understand the idea before making the object.

The studio portfolio brings together different kinds of making: a respiratory-system science model, UAE heritage miniature work, portrait drawings, calligraphy, canvas paintings and mural or interior visuals. Each project begins by clarifying what the finished work needs to communicate and how it will be used.

From there, the scope, materials, scale, finish and timeline can be discussed. The aim is to create work that feels considered, serves its purpose and is presented with care.

Studio principles

What guides the work.

Craft
