Portraits / Personalised gifts

Commissioning a custom portrait drawing as a personal gift

A thoughtful portrait commission starts with a clear reference photograph and a shared idea of the finished composition. Choose the image, size, number of people and gift date before requesting a quote.

At a glance

A thoughtful portrait commission starts with a clear reference photograph and a shared idea of the finished composition. Choose the image, size, number of people and gift date before requesting a quote.

Choose the photograph carefully

Use the highest-resolution original image available. The face should be in focus, with enough light to show the eyes and important features. Avoid screenshots, heavy filters and images that become pixelated when enlarged.

Decide what should appear in the drawing

Tell the studio whether the portrait should show a head-and-shoulders crop, a wider pose or more than one person. Explain which details matter most, such as an expression, hairstyle, clothing detail or a meaningful object.

When combining people from different photographs

Send each original image and explain the arrangement you have in mind. Combining separate references involves composition decisions, so the studio needs to review the images before confirming what can be achieved.

Plan the size and presentation

Think about where the portrait will be displayed and the available wall or frame size. Share the preferred orientation and any presentation preferences at the beginning so the drawing can be discussed in context.

Allow time for a project-specific schedule

Mention the occasion and the date the portrait is needed. Timing depends on the size, number of subjects, detail and current studio schedule. Do not assume a rush deadline can be accepted until it has been confirmed.

Request a portrait quote

Send the reference photograph, preferred size, number of people, style direction and target date to KALAM ARTS STUDIO in Abu Dhabi. The studio can review the requirements and discuss the artwork scope and handover arrangements.
