How to plan a custom mural or wall-art project in the UAE

A successful mural begins with the wall, the space and the purpose. Measure the surface, document its condition, agree the visual direction and check site permissions before scheduling the artwork.

At a glance

A successful mural begins with the wall, the space and the purpose. Measure the surface, document its condition, agree the visual direction and check site permissions before scheduling the artwork.

1. Photograph and measure the wall

Take a straight-on photograph of the full wall and note its width and height. Include doors, windows, sockets, furniture, signs and other features that affect the composition. A few wider room photographs help explain how people will view the finished artwork.

2. Describe the purpose of the artwork

A mural for a home may be planned around a personal subject or room palette. A café, clinic, salon or retail space may need artwork that supports the customer experience or existing brand identity. Explain the audience, mood and main message before choosing a style.

3. Check the surface and site conditions

Mention whether the wall is indoors or outdoors, what the surface is made from and whether it has cracks, moisture, peeling paint or other preparation needs. For an outdoor or high-traffic area, discuss exposure, access and maintenance expectations before choosing materials.

4. Confirm permissions and access

For rented, shared or managed premises, check the property owner, building management or venue requirements before work is scheduled. Clarify working hours, access equipment, protection of nearby surfaces and any event or business operating constraints.

5. Agree the design scope and timing

Share brand colours or a style reference if relevant. Confirm the wall dimensions, artwork coverage, design complexity, preparation requirements and target installation date. A project quote should reflect the actual site and scope rather than a wall photograph alone.
