Inside the studio

From sketch and drawing to fabricated, finished artwork

Depending on the brief, a project can move from a pencil sketch, reference layout or AutoCAD drawing to material planning, measured cutting, hand fabrication, painting and final review. The exact stages depend on the object, artwork and display requirements.

Plan a project with us ↗

How does a custom 3D craft project move from drawing to finished piece?

The studio first reviews the purpose, references and dimensions, then discusses a visual plan and suitable materials. Fabrication, assembly, painting and finishing follow according to the agreed scope, before the finished piece is reviewed for handover.

A clear creative journey

Each stage has a purpose.

The process is adapted to the subject, scale, material, display requirement and deadline.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Brief & reference

Clarify the purpose, audience, dimensions, quantity, reference images and delivery target.

02 / PLAN

Sketch & drawing

Develop the visual direction through a pencil sketch, layout or technical drawing. AutoCAD planning can be considered where the project requires it.

03 / MAKE

Fabrication

Select suitable materials and build the form through measured cutting, assembly, shaping and hands-on craft.

04 / FINISH

Painting & detail

Apply colour, surface details and finishing touches, then review the work against the agreed brief.

Made with attention

The result should work beautifully in real life.

We consider how the finished artwork or model will be viewed, transported, displayed or used. That practical thinking is part of the creative process—not an afterthought.

✓Clear scope — what is included, what is required and when it is needed.
✓Material suitability — choices guided by form, use and finish.
✓Detail review — proportion, colour, surface and presentation.
✓Handover planning — practical coordination around the finished work.
